we worked on a challenge that affects something we often take for granted—finding our way around a campus..

Our project focuses on campus accessibility, specifically making the next accessible route clear and easy to understand.

THE Challenge:the challenges faced by the new comers and visitor are when they enter the campus they wanted to go at some point but they are confused to find a clear accessiablity. 

Way Finding:this becomes difficult when there are multiple buildings,paths and floors..different users have inforemation about the way but this infrmation should be passed to everyone.

Accessibility:We Consider accessablity in 4 dimensions and they are > mobility, vision, hearing and information/language.

Exiting slutions: for this our team has came across some solutions like campus maps,step free route signs,brile directions and digital navigations...

Three way solution:for this we have a 3 way soln and they are smart sign ,hybrid signs,digital-first.

Prototype:in our prototype we have clear physical signs,accessablity cue and QR digital layer...

Six design rule :we use these 6 design rule to make the way finding better..like high contrast,short messages,familar icon,consistent terms,tactical opinon and no colour only clue...

By following these the visitors and new comers can find their way to campus way easier...
